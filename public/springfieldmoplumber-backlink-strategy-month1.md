# One-Month Backlink Strategy — springfieldmoplumber.com

**Site age**: ~3 months | **Current backlink profile**: 1 external link (nofollow, Pinterest) | **Site type**: Lead-gen/referral, no physical location

---

## The one rule this whole plan follows

You have **1 backlink today**. Jumping to 40-50 links in a month on a 3-month-old site is the single fastest way to get algorithmically suppressed — sudden unnatural velocity is exactly what Google's spam systems are tuned to catch, and lead-gen/local-service is one of the most heavily scrutinized niches for exactly this pattern. This plan targets **~18-22 net-new links across 4 weeks**, ramping gradually, weighted toward relevance over volume. Slower and real beats fast and fake here.

**Also off the table, because there's no physical location**: Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Chamber of Commerce, Angi, and any directory that verifies or displays a service address. Listing a fake address is a ToS violation on the directory's side and a spam-policy risk on Google's side (local business impersonation). Skip that whole category — it's not a gray area worth testing on a brand-new site.

---

## Week 0 (before link-building starts) — Fix what's already broken

Do this in parallel with Week 1, not before it, but don't skip it:

| Issue | Page(s) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Blocked by robots.txt | 1 page | Check `robots.txt` — confirm this is intentional (e.g. a thank-you/thank you page) or remove the disallow rule |
| Page with redirect (failed validation) | 4 pages | Redirect is either looping, chaining, or pointing somewhere invalid — check destination status codes |
| Not found (404) | 1 page | Either restore the page or 301 it to the closest live equivalent — don't leave it dangling |
| Duplicate without user-selected canonical | 1 page | Add a `rel=canonical` pointing to the preferred version |

Any backlink pointed at one of these pages this month is wasted. Fix first, link second.

## Week 1 — Foundation & re-activate what's dormant

Target: **4-5 links**

- **Reclaim/strengthen the Web 2.0 properties already built** (Google Sites, GitHub Pages, Blogger per your earlier work) — confirm they're still live, indexed, and linking to the correct current pages (not ones affected by the canonical/redirect issues above).
- **Social profile completion pass**: LinkedIn Company Page, Twitter/X, Facebook Page — each with a live link to the homepage. These are low-authority individually but establish the entity footprint search engines cross-reference.
- **1 resource-page outreach**: search `"Springfield MO" "home services" OR "helpful resources" inurl:links`, find 3-4 realistic targets (local blogs, community sites, HOA/neighborhood resource pages), pitch 1-2 for inclusion as a plumbing resource.

## Week 2 — Contextual relevance

Target: **5-6 links**

- **Guest post outreach (start, not land)**: identify 5-8 home-improvement, real estate, or Springfield-lifestyle blogs that accept guest content. Pitch 1 article idea each (e.g. "Signs Your Springfield Home Has Hidden Pipe Damage," "What Missouri Winters Do to Your Plumbing"). Expect a 10-20% response rate — this week is outreach, not publication.
- **HARO / Qwoted / Featured.com responses**: 3-5 pitches responding to journalist queries about home maintenance, water bills, DIY plumbing myths. Even 1 placement here is a strong, naturally-earned link.
- **Broken link building**: use Ahrefs/a free checker on 5-10 competitor or authority plumbing resource pages, find dead links, offer your relevant page as the replacement. Low hit rate but very clean links when they land.

## Week 3 — Convert outreach, add partnerships

Target: **4-5 links**

- **Follow up all Week 2 outreach** — most guest post and HARO wins land on the follow-up, not the first pitch.
- **Reciprocal/referral links from real local plumbers** — continuing the outreach your earlier work started. If any plumbing businesses are open to a referral-partner relationship, a link from their site ("Get more reviews / find customers via springfieldmoplumber.com") is highly relevant and hard for competitors to replicate. Keep anchor text natural, not exact-match.
- **1 local sponsorship** if budget allows: a Little League team, 5K run, or community event page. These typically link a sponsor's site without requiring a claimed business address — check each one's actual listing requirements first.

## Week 4 — Publish, consolidate, measure

Target: **4-5 links**

- **Publish whichever guest posts/HARO pieces have been accepted** — this is where Weeks 2-3's work actually turns into links.
- **1-2 more Web 2.0 or profile links** on platforms not yet used (Medium, About.me, etc.) — keep this category small; it's the easiest to overdo.
- **Internal link audit**: your 183 internal links are healthy, but check whether any of the new/rewritten service-area pages from your recent SEO work are still under-linked internally — new backlinks work better when they land on pages that are themselves well-supported internally.

## Anchor text mix (across the whole month)

| Type | Share | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | ~40% | "springfieldmoplumber.com," "Springfield MO Plumber" |
| Naked URL | ~20% | `springfieldmoplumber.com` |
| Generic | ~25% | "this local plumbing resource," "find a plumber here" |
| Partial-match | ~15% | "emergency plumbing help in Springfield" |

Avoid exact-match anchors ("emergency plumber Springfield MO") more than once or twice all month — on a site with only ~20 total links, an exact-match-heavy profile is conspicuous rather than helpful.

---

## Tracking

Keep a simple log per link: URL acquired, target page, anchor text, follow/nofollow, date live. At month-end, check Search Console's Links report again — you're looking for the "Total external links" count to move from 1 to roughly 18-22, and for "Top linking sites" to diversify beyond a single domain.
